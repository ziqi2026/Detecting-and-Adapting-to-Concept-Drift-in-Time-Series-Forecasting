Generated by experiments/uncertainty/run_escalation_sweep.py; do not edit by hand.
Values are seed mean ± sample SD (ddof=1) over the seeds processed.

## Protocol

- Score: previous half-hour absolute forecast error |y_(t-1) - forecast_(t-1)|, rounded to 6 decimals (MW).
- Rule: escalate when score > tau (strict); a step with score == tau is retained.
- Tau grid per (region, UQ method, seed): tau = k × R_cal, R_cal = calibration 90% conformal radius (2020-01-01 to 2020-02-29), k = 0.50, 0.55, ..., 3.00, plus -inf and +inf.
- Regimes: T5 full scope (event start through event end + 7 days) plus 'all'.
- Operating points: grid row whose review rate is closest to each target (ties: lower review rate, then smaller tau); the 90% point is the lowest review rate with retained coverage >= 0.90 (NaN if never reached).
- Streams processed: 10 seed/region interval files.

## Shared lagged score across uncertainty methods

The lag-1 absolute forecast error depends on the point forecast, not the uncertainty interval. Static, ACI, and AgACI therefore review the same observations for each configuration and seed at the same threshold.
The first TEST observation has a score of 0 and is retained. This assumes the previous half-hour's observed demand has arrived before the current prediction is issued.
ACI groups with more than two distinct review rates: 680 of 680.
