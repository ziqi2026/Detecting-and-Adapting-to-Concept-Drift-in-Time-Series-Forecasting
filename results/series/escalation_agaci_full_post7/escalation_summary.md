## Protocol

- Score: interval width (upper - lower), rounded to 6 decimals (MW).
- Rule: escalate when score > tau (strict); a step with score == tau is retained.
- Tau grid per (region, UQ method, seed): tau = k × W_cal, W_cal = 2 × calibration conformal radius (2020-01-01 to 2020-02-29), k = 0.50, 0.55, ..., 3.00, plus -inf and +inf.
- Regimes: T5 full scope (7-day buffers) plus 'all'.
- Operating points: grid row whose review rate is closest to each target (ties: lower review rate, then smaller tau); the 90% point is the lowest review rate with retained coverage >= 0.90 (NaN if never reached).
- Streams processed: 10 seed/region interval files.
