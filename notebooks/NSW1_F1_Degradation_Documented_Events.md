#### Documented Events Used in the NSW1 F1 Degradation Curve

The following AEMO-documented events are included as historical reference points when interpreting changes in NSW1 rolling forecasting error.

The events provide historical context only. They are not treated as confirmed concept drift events.

- **March–April 2020 — Early COVID-19 Demand Disruption:**  
  AEMO reported that changes to work, education, business activity and daily routines affected electricity demand across Australia. However, it was difficult to separate the COVID-19 impact from weather, seasonality, day-of-week effects and rooftop PV. March–April 2020 is therefore used as a broad context period rather than an exact NSW1 event period.

- **1 October 2021 — Five-Minute Settlement Commencement:**  
  Five-Minute Settlement commenced across the National Electricity Market on 1 October 2021. It aligned operational dispatch and financial settlement at five-minute intervals. This NEM-wide market change included New South Wales.

- **15–24 June 2022 — NEM Spot-Market Suspension:**  
  AEMO suspended the NEM spot market in all regions on 15 June 2022 because of exceptional market and operational conditions. The suspension ended at 2 pm AEST on 24 June 2022. This event included NSW1, but it was not a NSW-only event.

- **16 March 2023 — Direction to a NSW Generator:**  
  AEMO directed a New South Wales market participant to help maintain the power system in a reliable operating state during actual and forecast lack-of-reserve conditions in NSW.

- **17 March 2023 — New South Wales Market Suspension:**  
  AEMO recorded a New South Wales market suspension on 17 March 2023. This was a separate NSW-specific event from the generator direction issued on 16 March 2023.

- **27 October–25 November 2023 — Directions to SA and NSW Generators:**  
  AEMO issued several directions to South Australian and New South Wales market participants during this period. The directions were used to maintain power-system security. This event is relevant to NSW1 because the official report specifically includes New South Wales participants.
